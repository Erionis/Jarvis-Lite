# Brand assets

- `logo.svg` is the Jarvis Lite wordmark with a small `LITE` descriptor and no
  separate symbol.
- The letters `A` and `I` highlight the AI identity in E4 Teal `#007373`.
- The remaining letters use E4 Black `#1A171B`; the background is white
  `#FFFFFF`.

The wordmark uses Jost Semi 600 and the descriptor uses Jost Medium 500,
converted to outlines so the SVG requires no installed font and embeds no font
software. Jost is distributed under the
[SIL Open Font License 1.1](https://scripts.sil.org/OFL); the source used here
is the official
[`indestructible-type/Jost`](https://github.com/indestructible-type/Jost/tree/35f141c970538f1ed0f235789c19156b3ce2a762)
repository.

Keep the proportions, spacing, and color distinction between `AI` and the
remaining letters intact. For dark backgrounds, create a high-contrast variant
without changing the geometry.
