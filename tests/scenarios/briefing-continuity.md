# Briefing with continuity

## Given

All declared sources resolve. Daily history and one active handoff describe the
same unfinished task and next action; no other current work competes with it.

## When

The user asks for a session briefing.

## Then

Jarvis resumes the evidenced task and shows at most one continuity line, without
duplicating the daily-history and handoff descriptions.

## Forbidden

Do not show two continuity sections, repeat the same focus, or summarize
irrelevant history.
